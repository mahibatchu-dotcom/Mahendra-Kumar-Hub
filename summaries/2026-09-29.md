# A Vote of Confidence
*2026-09-29* · [Original article](https://www.dot.news/post/6aba834862068d00024d2821)

**In one line:** The Election Commission of India is facing public criticism over the deletion of 13 crore voters during a major list revision and reports of serious internal disagreements among its three commissioners.

**Key points:**
- The Election Commission of India (ECI) is carrying out a Special Intensive Revision (SIR) to rebuild the country's voter lists from scratch.
- Critics and politicians from both the opposition and ruling parties have alleged widespread irregularities, including fake entries and the wrongful removal of millions of voters.
- More than 13 crore names have reportedly been removed from the draft voter lists across India so far.
- Two Election Commissioners, Sukhbir Singh Sandhu and Vivek Joshi, have reportedly lodged 14 formal objections against decisions made by Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar.
- The two commissioners claim that major decisions, communications, and foreign trips were being authorized without their knowledge or approval.

**Numbers and names to remember:**
- **Gyanesh Kumar:** Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) of India.
- **Sukhbir Singh Sandhu and Vivek Joshi:** The two Election Commissioners who raised internal objections.
- **Rahul Gandhi:** Leader of the Opposition in the Lok Sabha.
- **Anurag Thakur:** BJP Member of Parliament.
- **Yogendra Yadav:** A critic who presented cases of living voters declared deceased.
- **June 2025:** When Phase I of the voter list revision began in Bihar.
- **13 crore:** The number of names reportedly removed from draft electoral rolls.
- **14:** The number of formal internal objections filed by the two Election Commissioners over 10 months.
- **Election Commission of India (ECI):** The constitutional body responsible for conducting elections in India.
- **Bihar, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka:** States where the voter list revision exercises and related controversies occurred.

**Why it matters:**
An accurate voter list is vital for free and fair elections, but mass voter removals and internal rifts at the top level of the Election Commission threaten public trust in India's democratic systems. 

**Key terms:**
- **Special Intensive Revision (SIR):** A process to rebuild and clean up the voter list from scratch, using door-to-door or online forms.
- **Electoral roll:** The official list of registered people who are eligible to vote.
- **Draft rolls:** Preliminary voter lists published for review and public feedback before being finalized.
- **Chief Election Commissioner (CEC):** The head of the Election Commission, who serves as the "first among equals" alongside two other commissioners.
