# Closing The Loop: A Threat From Inside
*2026-10-03* · [Original article](https://www.dot.news/post/6abfff3a1bb99e00025befb3)

**In one line:**
An Indian captain successfully landed a Flydubai flight in Saudi Arabia after being stabbed mid-flight by his co-pilot in an apparent hijack attempt.

**Key points:**
- Flydubai flight FZ 1073 from Dubai to Tel Aviv suddenly descended over 17,000 feet in less than two minutes after a hijacking alert was triggered.
- The flight's Indian captain, Smit Machchhar, was stabbed by his Omani co-pilot, who reportedly tried to take control and crash the plane.
- Despite his injuries, Captain Machchhar unlocked the cockpit, allowing passengers and off-duty pilots to subdue the attacker and safely land the aircraft.
- The rapid descent damaged the plane's tail rudder, but the pilots were still able to recover and land at Tabuk Airport in Saudi Arabia.
- Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu labeled the incident a terrorist attack, raising security concerns about foreign pilots from nations without diplomatic ties to Israel.

**Numbers and names to remember:**
- September 30: The date of the flight incident.
- FZ 1073: The Flydubai flight number.
- 170: The number of passengers on board.
- 17,000 feet: The height the plane descended in under two minutes.
- Smit Machchhar: The Indian captain of the flight who survived the stabbing.
- Tabuk Airport: The location in Saudi Arabia where the plane landed safely.
- Benjamin Netanyahu: The Prime Minister of Israel.
- Oman: The home country of the attacking co-pilot.

**Why it matters:**
The incident highlights the ongoing security vulnerability of modern cockpit doors, which can keep help out if a threat originates from a pilot inside. It also adds significant political and security tension in Israel ahead of its upcoming parliamentary elections.

**Key terms:**
- Squawk 7700: An international radio code signaling a general inflight emergency.
- Squawk 7500: An international radio code signaling a hijacking or unlawful interference.
- Knesset: The national parliament of Israel.
- Axis of Resistance: An informal political and military alliance in the Middle East that opposes Israel.
