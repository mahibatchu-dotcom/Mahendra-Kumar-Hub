# Red Label
*2026-10-13* · [Original article](https://www.dot.news/post/6acd105c42feba00025e904a)

**In one line:** India's food regulator has spent over a decade struggling to implement front-of-pack nutrition labels amid pushback from the food industry and criticism over delays.

**Key points:**
- The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) has proposed front-of-pack (FoP) labels to warn consumers about packaged foods high in salt, sugar, or fat.
- Non-communicable diseases linked to poor diets cause 63% of deaths in India, and adult obesity rates almost doubled between 2005–06 and 2023–24.
- Many packaged products sold in India contain poorer formulations—such as triple the sugar or cheaper oils—than the same brands sold in Britain or Europe.
- The push for FoP labels began in 2014 following a Delhi High Court order, but successive draft regulations were delayed or diluted.
- FSSAI proposed a five-star Indian Nutrition Rating system in 2022, but recently indicated it would withdraw it following disputes and public comments.
- The latest regulatory push comes amid pressure from a Supreme Court case brought by the non-profit 3S and Our Health Society.

**Numbers and names to remember:**
- **FSSAI:** Food Safety and Standards Authority of India, the country's food regulator.
- **WHO:** World Health Organization, which estimates non-communicable diseases cause 63% of Indian deaths.
- **3 crore:** The projected number of Indian children and adolescents living with obesity by 2030.
- **B. Sesikeran:** Former director of the National Institute of Nutrition who led a 2018 FSSAI committee while linked to food industry lobby group ILSI.
- **3S and Our Health Society:** The Kerala-based non-profit that petitioned the Supreme Court regarding clear food labelling.

**Why it matters:**
Clear front-of-pack labels can help consumers make healthier choices and combat a growing epidemic of diet-related chronic illnesses. However, years of regulatory delays and watered-down proposals have left public health advocates and industry at odds.

**Key terms:**
- **Front-of-pack (FoP) labels:** Prominent nutrition ratings or warning labels printed on the front of food packaging.
- **Health halo effect:** The misleading impression that a food product is healthy overall because of a single claim like "low-fat" or "high-fibre".
- **Non-communicable diseases (NCDs):** Chronic medical conditions that are not infectious, such as diabetes and heart disease.
