# Red Label: FSSAI’s front-of-pack labels satisfy neither health groups nor the food industry
*2026-10-14* · [Original article](https://www.dot.news/post/6acd105c42feba00025e904a)

**In one line:** India's food regulator, the FSSAI, has faced a decade of delays and criticism over proposed front-of-pack nutrition labels intended to combat unhealthy food consumption and rising non-communicable diseases.

**Key points:**
- The FSSAI recently proposed front-of-pack (FoP) labels to warn consumers when packaged foods are high in salt, sugar, or fat.
- Diets high in salt, sugar, and fat contribute to non-communicable diseases like diabetes and heart disease, which account for 63% of deaths in India.
- Packaged foods sold in India often have inferior formulations or higher sugar content compared to the same products sold in other global markets.
- India's journey toward FoP labelling began in 2014, but multiple committees, drafts, and proposed star-rating systems have faced delays, industry pushback, and criticism from public health groups.
- A current Supreme Court petition filed by a Kerala-based non-profit argues that the lack of clear food labelling threatens public health.

**Numbers and names to remember:**
- **FSSAI:** Food Safety and Standards Authority of India.
- **Revant Himatsingka (Food Pharmer):** Social media influencer who examines packaged food products.
- **B. Sesikeran:** Former director of the National Institute of Nutrition who chaired a 2018 FSSAI committee.
- **2030:** Expected timeframe for nearly 3 crore children and adolescents in India to live with obesity.
- **63%:** Percentage of all deaths in India accounted for by non-communicable diseases, according to the WHO.

**Why it matters:** 
Clear food labelling is critical in addressing India's rising rates of obesity and non-communicable diseases driven by processed foods. However, prolonged disagreements between regulators, food companies, and health advocates have left consumers without prominent warning labels.

**Key terms:**
- **Front-of-pack (FoP) labelling:** Nutrition labels placed on the front of food packets that are prominent and simple, designed to immediately alert consumers to high levels of salt, sugar, or fat.
- **Health halo effect:** The misleading impression created by marketing claims (such as "low-fat" or "multigrain") that makes a food product appear healthier overall than it actually is.
- **Non-communicable diseases (NCDs):** Chronic medical conditions, such as diabetes and heart disease, that are not passed from person to person.
