# Going Against the Tide
*2026-10-29* · [Original article](https://www.dot.news/post/6ae2179d256df600024c89b1)

**In one line:** Brazilian President Lula has won a historic fourth term after narrowly defeating Flávio Bolsonaro, who rejected the election results and fled to the United States.

**Key points:**
- Lula secured a fourth term as Brazil's president in a close election against Flávio Bolsonaro.
- Flávio Bolsonaro called for the election results to be annulled and fled to the United States while under investigation for money laundering.
- US President Donald Trump welcomed Flávio Bolsonaro and publicly criticized the Brazilian election results.
- Flávio's father, former President Jair Bolsonaro, is currently serving a 27-year prison sentence for attempting a coup after losing the 2022 election to Lula.
- Lula previously led Brazil during an economic boom, served 18 months in prison on bribery charges before his conviction was annulled, and returned to the presidency in 2022.

**Numbers and names to remember:**
- Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva (Lula): President of Brazil who won a fourth term.
- Flávio Bolsonaro: Presidential candidate who rejected the election outcome and fled to the US.
- Jair Bolsonaro: Former Brazilian president currently serving 27 years in prison for attempting a coup.
- Donald Trump: US President who backed the Bolsonaro family.
- Workers' Party (PT): The left-wing political party founded by Lula.
- Key figures/dates: 1988 (Brazil became a modern democracy), 2002 (Lula's first presidential win), ~4 crore (low-income Brazilians helped by Bolsa Família), 50.9% vs 49.1% (Lula's 2022 victory margin over Jair Bolsonaro), 27 years (Jair Bolsonaro's prison sentence).

**Why it matters:**
The election outcome demonstrates the continued political division in South America's largest economy. It also highlights ongoing threats to democratic stability from far-right leaders challenging election results.

**Key terms:**
- Workers' Party (PT): A left-wing political party in Brazil founded by Lula.
- Bolsa Família: A Brazilian government social program providing direct financial assistance to low-income households.
- Operation Car Wash: A major corruption investigation in Brazil that targeted political leaders, including Lula.
- Bolsonarism: The far-right, conservative political movement led by former President Jair Bolsonaro.
