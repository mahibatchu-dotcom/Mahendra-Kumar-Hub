# City of Scandal
*2026-11-05* · [Original article](https://www.dot.news/post/6ae8d2bc16f9b60002c9ed31)

**In one line:** Manchester City transformed into a dominant football powerhouse through massive financial backing, but now faces scrutiny over more than 100 alleged breaches of financial rules.

**Key points:**
- Former Thai Prime Minister Thaksin Shinawatra bought a majority stake in Manchester City in 2007, but his assets were frozen following corruption charges in Thailand.
- Sheikh Mansour bin Zayed Al Nahyan's Abu Dhabi United Group bought the club in September 2008, funding major player signings.
- Under heavy investment, Manchester City won over 20 trophies, including multiple Premier League titles and the Champions League.
- UEFA introduced Financial Fair Play (FFP) rules to stop clubs from spending more than they earn, which were later updated to Financial Sustainability Regulations (FSR).
- An independent commission found Manchester City guilty of more than 100 breaches of Premier League financial rules over nearly a decade, though the club denies wrongdoing.

**Numbers and names to remember:**
- Sheikh Mansour bin Zayed Al Nahyan, Thaksin Shinawatra, Sven-Göran Eriksson, Sir Alex Ferguson, Roberto Mancini, Pep Guardiola, Gary Cook, Roman Abramovich.
- Manchester City, Manchester United, Abu Dhabi United Group (ADUG), UEFA, Premier League, Chelsea.
- 100+ rule breaches, 2007, September 2008, 2009, 2011, 2017-18, €1.6 billion combined European losses in 2009, €5 million loss limit.

**Why it matters:**
The case brings national attention to financial fairness in modern football and questions whether ultra-wealthy owners give certain teams an unfair advantage.

**Key terms:**
- Transfer market: The process where football clubs buy and sell players.
- Financial Fair Play (FFP): UEFA regulations created to limit spending so clubs do not spend more than they earn.
- Financial Sustainability Regulations (FSR): Revised UEFA rules that limit squad spending to a specific percentage of a club's total revenue.
- Profitability and Sustainability Rules (PSR): Financial limits enforced by the Premier League.
