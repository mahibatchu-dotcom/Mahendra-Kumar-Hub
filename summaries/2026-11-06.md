# War and Peace and Everything in Between
*2026-11-06* · [Original article](https://www.dot.news/post/6ae8658fdfd5780002922dee)

**In one line:** The Russia-Ukraine war approaches its fifth year, rooted in centuries of shared history, political shifts, and opposing ideas of national identity.

**Key points:**
- Recent diplomatic moves include Norway and Finland calling Russian President Vladimir Putin, a 270-person prisoner swap, and a short ceasefire in Oleshky brokered by Melania Trump.
- Ukraine and Russia trace historical origins to Kievan Rus' in the 9th century, but Ukraine developed a distinct identity despite centuries of Russian conquest, Soviet rule, forced Russification, and the Holodomor famine.
- In 1991, 92% of Ukrainians voted for independence after the USSR collapsed, and Ukraine later surrendered 5,000 nuclear weapons in exchange for security guarantees.
- Protests during the 2004 Orange Revolution and the 2013 Euromaidan movement pushed Ukraine toward Western alignment, leading Russia to illegally annex Crimea in 2014 and back separatists in Donetsk and Luhansk.
- Russia launched a full-scale invasion in February 2022, creating a prolonged conflict between Russia's authoritarian ethnic nationalism and Ukraine's democratic civic nationalism.

**Why it matters:**
The conflict underscores a fundamental battle over sovereign identity between Russian imperial authority and Ukrainian democratic self-determination. Understanding this deep history explains why the war is so persistent and difficult to resolve.

**Key terms:**
- **Kievan Rus’:** A 9th-century kingdom covering parts of modern Ukraine, Russia, and Belarus.
- **Holodomor:** A 1930s Soviet man-made famine in Ukraine that killed millions.
- **Orange Revolution:** 2004 Ukrainian street protests triggered by a rigged presidential election.
- **Euromaidan:** 2013 protests in Kyiv after President Yanukovych rejected a trade deal with the EU.
- **Civic nationalism:** A national identity built on shared political values and democratic principles rather than ethnic or cultural heritage.
