# A Very Dotty Diwali
*2026-11-11* · [Original article](https://www.dot.news/post/6ae9b0f72574ce0002520a53)

**In one line:** Dot has announced holiday gift subscriptions alongside new interactive features, including story comments and a community tab called Dot Com.

**Key points:**
- The Dot team is taking a short break for Diwali until Wednesday.
- Users can buy a one-year Dot gift subscription for ₹699 with no auto-renewal.
- Gift subscriptions have a 12-month validity starting upon redemption and do not store names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
- Dot has launched a comments section on its stories to encourage constructive discussions.
- A new community tab named Dot Com curates conversations taking place across different Dot stories.
- Citing Jakob Nielsen’s 90:9:1 rule on online participation, Dot is encouraging readers who normally lurk to actively share their thoughts.

**Numbers and names to remember:**
- ₹699: price of a one-year gifted subscription to Dot.
- Wednesday: day the Dot team returns from the Diwali break.
- 90:9:1 rule: a participation principle proposed twenty years ago stating 90% of users lurk, 9% contribute occasionally, and 1% do almost all posting.
- Jakob Nielsen: researcher who formulated the 90:9:1 participation rule.
- Dot Com: the new tab dedicated to Dot's community conversations.

**Why it matters:** The platform is expanding into an interactive community, aiming to foster constructive discussions while offering privacy-focused gift access for readers.

**Key terms:**
- 90:9:1 rule: an observation that in most online communities, 90% of users only read, 9% contribute occasionally, and 1% generate most activity.
- Lurkers: community members who read content but do not actively participate or comment.
