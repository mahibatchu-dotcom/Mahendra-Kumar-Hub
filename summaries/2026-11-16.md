# When The Chips Are Down
*2026-11-16* · [Original article](https://www.dot.news/post/6af9c483aa14790002a43c2d)

**In one line:** NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang has stepped down after admitting to extramarital affairs amid allegations that one of the women involved was a Chinese spy.

**Key points:**
- NVIDIA co-founder and CEO Jensen Huang resigned after admitting to extramarital affairs.
- Reports allege that one of the women involved may have been a Chinese spy using a "honeytrap" tactic.
- NVIDIA's Board of Directors launched an independent investigation into national security concerns related to the allegations.
- NVIDIA became the world's leading AI chipmaker, briefly reaching a $5 trillion market valuation and controlling about 80% of the global AI chip market.
- The company's GPUs and proprietary CUDA software power data centers operated by major cloud providers to train foundational AI models.

**Numbers and names to remember:**
- $5 trillion: NVIDIA's peak market valuation.
- 80%: NVIDIA's approximate share of the global AI chip market.
- Jensen Huang: Co-founder and former CEO of NVIDIA.
- King Goujian of Yue and King Fuchai of Wu: Figures from the 5th-century BCE Chinese historical story illustrating the "beauty stratagem."
- Ilya Sutskever: OpenAI co-founder who built AlexNet using NVIDIA chips in 2012.
- Organizations: NVIDIA, OpenAI, Amazon AWS, Microsoft Azure, Apple.
- Locations: USA, China, Taiwan.

**Why it matters:**
Jensen Huang is a central figure in the global AI revolution, and his stepping down over potential espionage allegations carries major geopolitical implications for the AI competition between the US and China.

**Key terms:**
- Meiren ji (honeytrap): An ancient tactic of using romantic or sexual distraction to compromise an enemy.
- GPU (Graphics Processing Unit): A chip designed to perform specialized mathematical tasks quickly and in parallel.
- CPU (Central Processing Unit): A general-purpose computer chip designed to manage a wide variety of tasks.
- CUDA: NVIDIA's proprietary software platform that enables general software code to run directly on its GPUs.
- Hyperscalers: Large cloud technology providers, like Amazon AWS and Microsoft Azure, that operate massive data centers.
