# Malignant Markup
*2026-11-17* · [Original article](https://www.dot.news/post/6afb23c9f06ac700027d9e44)

**In one line:** The Indian government has drastically reduced prices for many anti-cancer drugs following Supreme Court criticism, though some expensive treatments remain unaffected.

**Key points:**
- The government lowered prices for 137 anti-cancer drug formulations, with half seeing discounts of over 50% and the largest cut reaching 96%.
- The price reduction followed a Supreme Court hearing where judges criticized huge markups on cancer medicines as "extortion."
- Cancer is caused by uncontrolled cell growth due to DNA damage, which can be random, hereditary, or caused by external factors like carcinogens.
- Treatments include traditional methods like surgery, radiation, and chemotherapy, alongside modern options like hormone therapy, targeted therapy, and immunotherapy.
- Cancer treatment often leads to severe financial hardship for families in India, highlighted by the legal battle initiated by late patient Sarojam Radhakrishnan.

**Numbers and names to remember:**
- 137: The number of anti-cancer drug formulations with reduced prices.
- 96%: The percentage of the largest price cut.
- 1 in 10: The proportion of Indians who will develop cancer in their lifetime, according to the WHO.
- 1 in 15: The proportion of Indians who will succumb to cancer, according to the WHO.
- ₹63,000: The monthly cost of medicines for patient Sarojam Radhakrishnan.
- Sarojam Radhakrishnan: A retired bank clerk from Kerala who fought high drug prices in court before passing away in September 2022.

**Why it matters:** High costs for cancer medicines cause severe financial ruin for families in India. Government price cuts relieve some of this burden, but the exclusion of certain expensive drugs leaves significant challenges for patients.

**Key terms:**
- Metastasis: The spreading of abnormal cancer cells to distant organs in the body.
- Carcinogens: Cancer-causing factors, which can be chemicals (like tobacco), physical radiation, or infections.
- Chemotherapy: A conventional cancer treatment that kills both cancer cells and healthy cells indiscriminately.
- Immunotherapy: Medicine that helps the body's immune system recognize and destroy cancer cells by removing fake signals used by the disease.
