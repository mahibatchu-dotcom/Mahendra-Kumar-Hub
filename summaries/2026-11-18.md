# Caught Red-Handed
*2026-11-18* · [Original article](https://www.dot.news/post/6afc3ddbfac3ca0002679767)

**In one line:** Prime Minister Narendra Modi declared that Naxalism has effectively ended in India after six decades of insurgency, as no Indian district remains classified as Left-Wing Extremism affected.

**Key points:**
- West Singhbhum district in Jharkhand was the last district in India classified as affected by Left-Wing Extremism (LWE).
- The national flag was hoisted at Sangajata village in the Saranda forest of Jharkhand on Independence Day, possibly for the first time ever.
- The government informed Parliament that while no district is currently LWE-affected, 38 districts are under a "Legacy and Thrust" category to prevent a resurgence.
- The Naxalite movement originated in 1967 in Naxalbari, West Bengal, when communist leader Charu Mazumdar led a small farmer land uprising.
- Naxalites adopt Maoist ideology and guerrilla warfare to violently challenge the Indian state, driven by grievances over land, resource extraction, and the displacement of tribal populations.

**Numbers and names to remember:**
- Narendra Modi: Prime Minister of India who declared the end of Naxalism.
- Charu Mazumdar: Communist leader who led the 1967 uprising in Naxalbari.
- Mao Zedong: Developer of Maoism and the military strategy known as the "Protracted People's War".
- Saranda Forest & Sangajata village: Asia's largest Sal forest and the village in West Singhbhum, Jharkhand, where the flag was hoisted.
- 1967: The year the Naxal movement began.
- 38: The number of districts currently listed under the "Legacy and Thrust" category.

**Why it matters:**
The resolution of the Naxalite-Maoist insurgency marks a historic shift in India's internal security after nearly 60 years of violent conflict. It opens up long-isolated tribal regions to security, formal administration, and potential economic development.

**Key terms:**
- Left-Wing Extremism (LWE): The Indian government's official classification for radical communist and Maoist insurgencies.
- Naxalite / Maoist: Followers of a radical form of communism that advocates armed, violent revolution by peasants and workers to overthrow the government.
- Adivasis: Indigenous tribal communities in India.
- Legacy and Thrust: A official category for regions where LWE has been brought under control but still requires monitoring to prevent a comeback.
- Protracted People's War: A long-term guerrilla warfare strategy developed by Mao Zedong to slowly weaken a stronger government state.
