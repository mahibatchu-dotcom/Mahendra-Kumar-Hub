# How the Swiss cheese model explains Microsoft's massive outage.
*2026-11-20* · [Original article](https://www.dot.news/post/6aff042ffe3d610002ad5f8c)

**In one line:** A global Microsoft 365 outage lasting nearly 24 hours disrupted services worldwide after software flaws and server traffic rerouting combined to paralyze key systems.

**Key points:**
- Microsoft 365 experienced a global outage lasting almost 24 hours on Thursday, November 12, and Friday, November 13.
- The failure disrupted hospitals, police departments, jails, government operations, and automated systems like pool chemical warnings at a German hotel.
- The cost of lost productivity from the single-day outage is estimated at around $7 billion.
- A software update applied four months earlier in July introduced a bug called "lock contention" on Microsoft's newest and fastest gateway servers.
- The bug was missed during lab testing because Microsoft tested the software on older, slower computers.
- Automated systems closed a secondary security gate (Gate B) and redirected all Microsoft Teams traffic through the main email gate (Gate A), causing the system to freeze.

**Numbers and names to remember:**
- **Microsoft 365 (formerly Office 365):** Microsoft's suite of apps including Outlook, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams, and Copilot AI.
- **Over 40 crore (400 million):** Number of people worldwide who use Microsoft 365.
- **November 12 and November 13:** Dates of the outage.
- **Nearly 24 hours:** Duration of the service disruption.
- **$7 billion:** Estimated monetary value of lost productivity caused by the outage.
- **Fiji:** Country whose annual GDP equals the estimated damage from the outage.
- **Germany:** Location of a hotel affected by an undelivered chlorine warning email during the outage.
- **Networking Gateway / Exchange Online:** Microsoft's traffic checkpoint servers and main email storage vault.

**Why it matters:**
The event highlights the extreme global dependence on centralized tech infrastructure, where minor software glitches can cascade into massive disruptions affecting public safety, healthcare, and billions of dollars in economic activity.

**Key terms:**
- **Microsoft 365:** A suite of cloud-based productivity and communication software tools.
- **Server:** A specialized computer without a screen or keyboard that stores data and processes requests in data centers.
- **Networking Gateway:** A system that verifies user logins and directs internet requests to Microsoft's core servers.
- **Exchange Online:** Microsoft's cloud server system where emails, attachments, and calendar data are stored.
- **Lock contention:** A bug where multiple computer processes attempt to access the same shared resource simultaneously, causing systems to freeze.
- **Swiss cheese model:** A scenario where multiple minor, independent failures line up to cause a major disaster.
