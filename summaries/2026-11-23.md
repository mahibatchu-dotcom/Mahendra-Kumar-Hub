# Privacy vs Press Freedom: As the DPDP Act advances, news publishers take their concerns to the Supreme Court
*2026-11-23* · [Original article](https://www.dot.news/post/6b0294304a1a1d00029ee745)

**In one line:** Indian digital news publishers have approached the Supreme Court to pause certain provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, arguing it could harm journalism and force newsrooms to shut down.

**Key points:**
- The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology recently opened registration for Consent Managers under the DPDP Act, 2023.
- Major digital news outlets like Network18, NDTV, and The Indian Express are seeking a stay on specific parts of the Act.
- The DPDP Act regulates how companies collect and use personal data and introduces strict rules for user consent and data deletion.
- The law introduces the "Right to Erasure" and establishes neutral third-party platforms called Consent Managers to let users manage their data permissions.
- Companies failing to follow the rules face heavy penalties of up to ₹250 crore, enforced by the Data Protection Board of India.

**Numbers and names to remember:**
- November 13: The date MeitY opened registration for Consent Managers.
- Justice K.S. Puttaswamy: The retired judge who approached the Supreme Court in 2012 over Aadhaar privacy safeguards.
- Justice B.N. Srikrishna: Led the committee that drafted the initial 2018 data protection bill.
- ₹250 crore: The maximum penalty for companies that violate the DPDP Act.
- Organisations: Network18, NDTV, The Indian Express, MeitY, and the Data Protection Board of India.

**Why it matters:** The DPDP Act represents a major overhaul of digital privacy and data regulations in India, but it has sparked a conflict between safeguarding citizen privacy and protecting press freedom for journalists and newsrooms.

**Key terms:**
- **DPDP Act, 2023:** Digital Personal Data Protection Act; India's law regulating how companies collect and handle personal data.
- **Data Principal:** The individual whose personal data is being collected.
- **Data Fiduciary:** The company or organization that collects and processes personal data.
- **Consent Manager:** A neutral third-party platform providing a single dashboard for users to manage their data consents.
- **GDPR:** General Data Protection Regulation; Europe's data privacy law, often seen as a global gold standard.
