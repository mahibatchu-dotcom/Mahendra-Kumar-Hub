# A COP-Out: COP31 ends without a climate deal!
*2026-11-24* · [Original article](https://www.dot.news/post/6b03e213e069570002453223)

**In one line:** The COP31 UN climate conference in Türkiye ended without a final agreement after countries failed to reach a consensus on phasing out fossil fuels.

**Key points:**
- COP31 became the longest UN climate conference in history, going two days into overtime talks.
- The summit collapsed because countries could not agree on the overarching deal called the "Antalya Pact."
- The main sticking point was the refusal of some countries to name fossil fuels in the agreement.
- Fossil fuels are responsible for nearly 70% of all greenhouse gas emissions and are the leading cause of global warming.
- Disagreements persist between nations over who bears the responsibility and should move first to reduce emissions.

**Numbers and names to remember:**
- **COP31:** The climate meet held in Türkiye.
- **Antalya Pact:** The overarching deal that failed to gain consensus.
- **2009 Denmark Summit:** The last time a major climate summit ended in a similar collapse.
- **1.5°C / 2°C:** Global warming limits set by the Paris Agreement.
- **Türkiye, Australia, UAE, China, US, India, Saudi Arabia, Japan, Germany, UK:** Countries mentioned regarding emissions, hosting, or historical impact.

**Why it matters:** The failure of COP31 highlights deep global divisions over climate responsibilities and threatens international progress on cutting emissions just as scientists warn the world is nearing critical temperature limits.

**Key terms:**
- **COP:** Conference of the Parties, the annual UN meeting of roughly 200 countries to discuss climate change.
- **Fossil fuels:** Carbon-rich energy sources like coal, oil, and natural gas formed from ancient plants and animals.
- **Greenhouse gas:** Gases like carbon dioxide that trap heat in the earth's atmosphere and cause global warming.
- **NDCs (Nationally Determined Contributions):** National climate targets that every country sets and updates under the Paris Agreement.
