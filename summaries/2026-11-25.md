# Grand Turmoil Afoot
*2026-11-25* · [Original article](https://www.dot.news/post/6b0435fc3730c00002c6d096)

**In one line:** Although the video game industry generates massive revenue through blockbuster releases, it is currently struggling with widespread layoffs, studio closures, and financial instability.

**Key points:**
- Grand Theft Auto VI made $3.8 billion in its first 24 hours, earning more than the highest-grossing film, Avengers: Endgame.
- Overall video game sales are larger than the film and recorded music industries combined.
- Despite huge earnings, major companies are laying off thousands of employees and closing long-running studios.
- The industry previously survived a major crash in 1983 caused by rushed, low-quality games and competition from home computers.
- Gaming history led to the "Big Three" console makers: Nintendo, Sony, and Microsoft.
- The shift to digital stores like Steam, PlayStation, and Xbox stores has lowered physical manufacturing costs for publishers.

**Numbers and names to remember:**
- **$3.8 billion:** Revenue generated by GTA 6 in its first 24 hours.
- **$2.9 billion:** Total worldwide box office earned by Avengers: Endgame.
- **1983:** Year of the North American video game crash.
- **Big Three:** Console makers Nintendo, Sony, and Microsoft.
- **Rockstar, BioWare, Xbox, and Bluepoint Games:** Gaming companies affected by layoffs or studio closures.

**Why it matters:**
The contrast between record-breaking sales figures and widespread job cuts highlights severe structural challenges within the world's largest entertainment market.

**Key terms:**
- **Lockout chip:** Hardware introduced by Nintendo to block unauthorized games from running on its system.
- **Razor-and-blades business model:** Selling consoles at low profits or at a loss to make money from licensing fees on games.
- **Performance capture:** Technology used to record actors' movements and voice acting to make game characters realistic.
