# Green Cards and Red Tape
*2026-11-27* · [Original article](https://www.dot.news/post/6b0857f9eaabba00022c53f2)

**In one line:** The U.S. government has suspended PERM green card processing for major tech companies and proposed massive fees on H-1B visas to curb foreign worker programs.

**Key points:**
- The U.S. government suspended the Permanent Labour Certification Programme (PERM) for several major IT companies, including Microsoft, TCS, Infosys, and Cognizant.
- U.S. Vice President JD Vance accused companies like Microsoft of replacing laid-off American workers with foreign employees.
- The U.S. Department of Homeland Security proposed an additional fee of $103,265 on certain H-1B visa petitions to recover government costs.
- Indian nationals represent 70% of all H-1B visa applicants, making India the largest beneficiary of the program.
- The suspension of PERM processing delays the green card process for foreign workers, though it does not invalidate existing H-1B visas.

**Numbers and names to remember:**
- Figures: $103,265 (~₹95 lakh) proposed fee, $100,000 (~₹90 lakh) previously blocked fee, $215 (~₹20,000) former registration fee, 85,000 annual H-1B visa cap, 70% Indian applicants, 14% Chinese applicants, $8.8 billion estimated annual fee revenue.
- People: JD Vance (U.S. Vice President), Donald Trump (U.S. President).
- Organisations: U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS), Microsoft, Infosys, TCS, Wipro, HCL, Capgemini, Cognizant, Adobe, Google, Apple, Amazon, American Airlines.
- Places: United States, India, China.

**Why it matters:**
These policy shifts create major hurdles for Indian IT professionals and global tech companies relying on foreign talent to operate in the U.S. It also significantly delays the path to permanent U.S. residency for skilled foreign workers.

**Key terms:**
- H-1B visa: A temporary U.S. work visa for foreign professionals with specialized skills.
- Green card: A permit giving a foreign national the right to live and work in the U.S. permanently.
- PERM: A U.S. government certification process that employers must complete before sponsoring an employee for a green card.
- Non-immigrant visa: A visa allowing a person to enter the U.S. temporarily for work, study, or travel.
